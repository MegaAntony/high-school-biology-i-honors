Recommended 6-Step Study Sequence

Step-by-Step
1
Audit & Complete Lab Packet Pages 1–17 (Due Wed/Thu Oct 28–29)

Check your answers against the Part 2 Master Worked Keys (Pages 1–17) for Catalase liver reusability, ATP candy model, Poison case diagnostic table, Coral bleaching, and Algae beads.

2
Lock In the Verified 9-Step Bicarbonate Indicator pH Scale (p. 16–17)

Verified against Lab Packet p. 16: yellow = pH 7.6, orange = pH 8.0, red = pH 8.4 (equilibrium CO₂ ~0.04%), magenta = pH 8.8, dark purple = pH 9.2. Dark vial = respiration adds CO₂ (acidic); Light vial = photosynthesis removes CO₂ (alkaline).

3
Master the Comparison Matrix & 36–38 ATP Accounting

Contrast matrix/cristae vs. thylakoid/stroma, electron carriers (NADH/FADH₂ vs. NADPH), H⁺ pumping direction, electron source (glucose vs. water photolysis), and final electron acceptor (O₂ vs. NADP⁺). Note: Cellular respiration produces 36–38 ATP per glucose (32–34 from ETC oxidative phosphorylation, 2 from glycolysis, 2 from Krebs) matching Dr. Irwin's official keys.

4
Review Stations 1–8 Handout & Drill the 4 Poisons

Review active sites, Ea energy curves, and the 4 poisons: 2-DG (blocks glycolysis), Arsenic (blocks Krebs), Cyanide (blocks ETC Complex IV), and Oligomycin (blocks ATP Synthase).

5
Drill the 25-Question MCQ Simulator & 12-Question Deep-Dive Quizzes

Practice on the Summative Study Portal (25 MCQs), and test yourself with the 12-question scoreable quizzes in both the Cellular Respiration and Photosynthesis study guides under test conditions.

6
Rehearse Short Answer Blueprints 1–3 from Memory (Target: Monday, Nov 2)

Write out Blueprint 1 (Carbon/electron/energy flow), Blueprint 2 (Substrate vs. oxidative phosphorylation and role of O2), and Blueprint 3 (Light reactions & Calvin cycle coupling).
