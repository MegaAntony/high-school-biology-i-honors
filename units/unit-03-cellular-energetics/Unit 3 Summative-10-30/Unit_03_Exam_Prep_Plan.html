Quick facts to know cold

TopicFact
EnzymesLower activation energy; are not used up; do not change the energy difference between reactants and products.
InhibitorsCompetitive: binds the active site, can be overcome by more substrate. Non-competitive (allosteric): binds elsewhere and changes the enzyme's shape.
FermentationMakes NAD⁺ so glycolysis can keep running; no new ATP of its own (glycolysis gives 2 net ATP).
GlycolysisUsed by all living cells; no oxygen or mitochondria needed.
Krebs cycleMakes electron carriers (6 NADH and 2 FADH₂ per glucose) for the ETC.
Light reactionsPSII splits water; ETC 1 builds the H⁺ gradient that drives ATP synthase; PSI with ETC 2 makes NADPH.
Calvin cycleIn the stroma; RuBisCO fixes CO₂; ATP and NADPH reduce 3-PGA to G3P.
