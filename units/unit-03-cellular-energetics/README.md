## 🧭 Unit Structure & Segments

Unit 3 is organized into three interconnected sub-units under **GSE Standard SB1.e** (*Ask questions to investigate and provide explanations about the role of photosynthesis and respiration in the cycling of matter and flow of energy within the cell*):
* **Unit 3A — Enzymes & ATP-ADP Cycle:** Protein catalysts, active site specificity (Lock-and-Key vs. Induced Fit), activation energy ($E_a$), temperature/pH denaturation vs. cold slowing, substrate saturation ($V_{\max}$), competitive vs. non-competitive (allosteric) inhibition, and ATP hydrolysis/synthesis coupling.
* **Unit 3B — Cellular Respiration & Fermentation:** Glycolysis (cytosol), Pyruvate Oxidation (mitochondrial matrix), Krebs/Citric Acid Cycle (matrix), Electron Transport Chain & Chemiosmosis (inner mitochondrial membrane/cristae), Lactic Acid vs. Alcoholic Fermentation ($\text{NAD}^+$ regeneration), and the Mitochondrial Poison Case Study (2-Deoxyglucose, Arsenic, Cyanide, Oligomycin).
* **Unit 3C — Photosynthesis:** Chloroplast structure (thylakoid membrane, granum, stroma, stomata), Light-Dependent Reactions (Photosystem II water photolysis $\to$ ETC 1 $\to$ ATP Synthase; Photosystem I $\to$ ETC 2 $\to$ $\text{NADP}^+$ Reductase $\to$ NADPH), Calvin Cycle (RuBisCO carbon fixation, reduction to G3P, RuBP regeneration), Coral Bleaching STEM Case, and the Algae Bead Bicarbonate Indicator Lab.

## 🧪 Verified Lab 5 Bicarbonate Indicator pH & Color Scale (`Unit 3 Lab Packet 26-27.pdf`, Pages 16–17)

Directly verified against the 9-vial colorimetric chart and text on **Page 16** of [`Unit 3 Lab Packet 26-27.pdf`](materials/pdf/Unit%203%20Lab%20Packet%2026-27.pdf):

> *"When it is red it shows that it contains a concentration of $\text{CO}_2$ that is equivalent to the concentration of $\text{CO}_2$ in the atmosphere ($0.04\%$). When there is more $\text{CO}_2$ in the indicator, it will turn from red through orange to yellow. When $\text{CO}_2$ is removed from the indicator solution, it will turn from red through magenta to dark purple."*

| Vial 1 | Vial 2 | Vial 3 | Vial 4 | Vial 5 ($0.04\%\text{ Atm. CO}_2$) | Vial 6 | Vial 7 | Vial 8 | Vial 9 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **yellow** | *(yellow-orange)* | **orange** | *(red-orange)* | **red** | *(red-magenta)* | **magenta** | *(magenta-purple)* | **purple** |
| **$\text{pH } 7.6$** | **$\text{pH } 7.8$** | **$\text{pH } 8.0$** | **$\text{pH } 8.2$** | **$\text{pH } 8.4$** | **$\text{pH } 8.6$** | **$\text{pH } 8.8$** | **$\text{pH } 9.0$** | **$\text{pH } 9.2$** |

* **Dark / Aluminum-Foil Vial:** Photosynthesis stops (`OFF`), while Cellular Respiration continues (`ON`), adding $\text{CO}_2$ to the solution $\longrightarrow$ indicator shifts from **red ($\text{pH } 8.4$)** through **orange ($\text{pH } 8.0$)** to **yellow ($\text{pH } 7.6$)**.
* **Bright Light Uncovered Vial:** Both processes occur, but **Rate of Photosynthesis $>$ Rate of Cellular Respiration**, causing a net removal of $\text{CO}_2$ $\longrightarrow$ indicator shifts from **red ($\text{pH } 8.4$)** through **magenta ($\text{pH } 8.8$)** to **dark purple ($\text{pH } 9.2$)**.
