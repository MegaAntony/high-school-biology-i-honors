## Area details

**1. Enzymes** — Can you say, without notes: protein catalyst; lowers Ea; no change in ΔG; not consumed; lock-and-key vs induced fit; "denatured", not "killed"? Draw the energy-hill graph with and without enzyme.

**2. Graphs** — For each of 4 graphs state shape and reason: temp (rises, peak, crashes: denatured; cold only slows), pH (bell, each enzyme own optimum), substrate (rises then plateau: saturation), enzyme conc. (rate rises linearly if substrate is plentiful).

**3. Inhibition** — Fill a 3-row table from memory: binding site, overcome by more substrate?, effect on Vmax. Competitive: active site, yes, same Vmax. Noncompetitive: allosteric site, no, lower Vmax.

**4. ATP** — 3 phosphates; hydrolysis releases energy (exergonic); ADP + Pi → ATP needs energy (endergonic); coupling powers work (e.g. pumps, muscle).

**5. Respiration** — Make a table: stage, location, inputs, outputs. Glycolysis (cytoplasm, 2 net ATP), pyruvate oxidation (matrix), Krebs (matrix, CO2), ETC (inner membrane, most ATP, O2 → H2O). Know why cristae are folded.

**6. Fermentation** — Purpose: regenerate NAD+. Lactic acid vs ethanol + CO2. No extra ATP.

**7. Poisons** — Memorize the 4-row table (2-deoxyglucose, arsenic, oligomycin, cyanide) against pyruvate / H+ / NADH readings. Practice: given a lab result, name the poison.

**8. Photosynthesis** — Equation, chloroplast, light reactions vs Calvin cycle, how it mirrors respiration.

**9. Practice** — Take `unit3-quiz1-trainer.html (in this folder)` timed. Redo any topic you missed twice.
