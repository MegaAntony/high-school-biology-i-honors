Unit 3: Cellular Energetics & Enzymes (Master Hub)

Biology I Honors • South Forsyth High School • Dr. Sarah Irwin (Room #371) • Standard: GSE SB1.e

🧭 Unit Structure & Segments

Unit 3 is organized into three interconnected sub-units under GSE Standard SB1.e (Ask questions to investigate and provide explanations about the role of photosynthesis and respiration in the cycling of matter and flow of energy within the cell): * Unit 3A — Enzymes & ATP-ADP Cycle: Protein catalysts, active site specificity (Lock-and-Key vs. Induced Fit), activation energy ($E_a$), temperature/pH denaturation vs. cold slowing, substrate saturation ($V_{\max}$), competitive vs. non-competitive (allosteric) inhibition, and ATP hydrolysis/synthesis coupling. * Unit 3B — Cellular Respiration & Fermentation: Glycolysis (cytosol), Pyruvate Oxidation (mitochondrial matrix), Krebs/Citric Acid Cycle (matrix), Electron Transport Chain & Chemiosmosis (inner mitochondrial membrane/cristae), Lactic Acid vs. Alcoholic Fermentation ($\text{NAD}^+$ regeneration), and the Mitochondrial Poison Case Study (2-Deoxyglucose, Arsenic, Cyanide, Oligomycin). * Unit 3C — Photosynthesis: Chloroplast structure (thylakoid membrane, granum, stroma, stomata), Light-Dependent Reactions (Photosystem II water photolysis $\to$ ETC 1 $\to$ ATP Synthase; Photosystem I $\to$ ETC 2 $\to$ $\text{NADP}^+$ Reductase $\to$ NADPH), Calvin Cycle (RuBisCO carbon fixation, reduction to G3P, RuBP regeneration), Coral Bleaching STEM Case, and the Algae Bead Bicarbonate Indicator Lab.
