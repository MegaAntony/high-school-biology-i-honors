## 🧠 Locked-In Core Facts (Walk-In Review)

### 🔢 1. The Numbers to Remember
* **3 - 2 - 1 No-K:** Sodium-Potassium pump exports **3 Na⁺ OUT**, imports **2 K⁺ IN**, and hydrolyzes **1 ATP**.
* **0.9% Saline:** Normal mammalian blood plasma is **0.9% salt (99.1% water)** = **Isotonic**.
* **0 ATP:** All passive transport (**Simple Diffusion, Facilitated Diffusion, Osmosis**) uses **ZERO energy** and moves **HIGH → LOW**.
* **ATP Required:** All active transport (**Pumps, Phagocytosis, Pinocytosis, Exocytosis**) requires **ATP** and moves **LOW → HIGH**.

### 💧 2. What Happens to Cells in Solutions?
* **Hypotonic (Pure / Distilled water):** Animal cells **lyse / burst**. Plant cells become **turgid** (firm, healthy; cell wall prevents burst).
* **Isotonic (0.9% Saline):** Animal cells are **normal** (dynamic equilibrium: net flow = 0). Plant cells are **flaccid** (limp).
* **Hypertonic (Saltwater / Syrup):** Animal cells undergo **crenation** (shrivel). Plant cells undergo **plasmolysis** (membrane shrinks from cell wall; wilts).

### 🧩 3. Membrane Proteins: TRACIE
* **T** – **Transport** (channels, carrier pumps)
* **R** – **Receptors** (signal transduction)
* **A** – **Anchorage** (cytoskeleton and ECM)
* **C** – **Cell Recognition** (glycoproteins, ABO markers)
* **I** – **Intercellular Joinings** (tight, gap junctions)
* **E** – **Enzymatic Activity** (metabolic active sites)
