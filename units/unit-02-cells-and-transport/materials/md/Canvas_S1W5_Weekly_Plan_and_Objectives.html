🎯 Official Unit Success Criteria (By End of Week)

  1. Identify and label all components of the phospholipid bilayer cell membrane.
  2. Provide a verbal or written explanation of how the structure of membrane components allows selective transport of different molecule types.
  3. Distinguish between passive and active transport given diagrams, properties, or real-world physiological scenarios.
  4. Compare and contrast prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells (nuclear membrane, organelle compartmentalization, size, circular vs. linear DNA).
  5. Compare and contrast plant and animal cells (cell wall, chloroplasts, central vacuole, centrioles, lysosomes).
  6. Identify organelles based on functional descriptions or microscopic/diagrammatic visual representations.
  7. Explain homeostasis: Provide verbal or written explanation of how transport mechanisms contribute to stable internal conditions with named examples.
