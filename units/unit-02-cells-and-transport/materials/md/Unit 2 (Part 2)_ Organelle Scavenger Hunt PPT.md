## 🧭 Overview & Core Systems Concept
In eukaryotic cells, organelles are connected structurally and functionally through the **endomembrane system**. Vesicles bud off from or fuse with other membrane-bound organelles to assist in the synthesis, storage, modification, and export of macromolecules (lipids and proteins).

* **Endomembrane System Members:** Endoplasmic Reticulum (Rough & Smooth), Golgi apparatus, Lysosomes, Vacuoles/Vesicles, and Plasma Membrane.

---

## 🧱 Complete Organelle Inventory

### 1. Cytoskeleton
* **Structure:** Network of threadlike protein fibers throughout the cytoplasm.
  * **Microfilaments:** Thin actin filaments; maintain cell shape and drive muscle contraction.
  * **Microtubules:** Hollow tubulin cylinders; maintain cell shape, form tracks for organelle movement, and move chromosomes during cell division (spindle fibers).
  * **Intermediate Filaments:** Intermediate diameter; reinforce cell shape and anchor the nucleus.
* **Function:** Reinforces cell shape, anchors organelles, enables cell motility.

---

### 2. Plasma Membrane
* **Structure:** Semipermeable phospholipid bilayer studded with proteins, carbohydrates, and cholesterol.
* **Function:** Controls what enters and exits the cell; provides protection; recognizes extracellular signals. Found in **ALL cells**.

---

### 3. Cytosol / Cytoplasm
* **Structure:** Jelly-like aqueous fluid containing dissolved ions, nutrients, and proteins.
  * $	ext{Cytoplasm} = 	ext{Cytosol} + 	ext{Organelles suspended in it}$.
* **Function:** Holds organelles in place; provides an aqueous medium where metabolic chemical reactions occur. Found in **ALL cells**.

---

### 4. Nucleus
* **Structure:** Enclosed by a double-membrane **nuclear envelope** perforated with nuclear pores. Contains genetic material (**DNA** as chromatin).
* **Function:** Protects and stores DNA; directs all cell activities and gene expression.
* *Note:* **NOT** present in prokaryotes (bacteria have a non-membrane-bound *nucleoid region*).

---

### 5. Nucleolus
* **Structure:** Dense, dark-staining region located *inside* the nucleus.
* **Function:** Transcribes ribosomal RNA (**rRNA**) and synthesizes ribosome subunits, which are exported to the cytoplasm for assembly.

---

### 6. Ribosomes
* **Structure:** Complexes composed of rRNA and proteins; consist of a large and small subunit. **Not membrane-bound**. Found in **ALL cells**.
* **Function:** Reads mRNA instructions to synthesize proteins.
  * **Bound Ribosomes (on Rough ER):** Synthesize proteins destined for membrane insertion, lysosomal packaging, or **export/secretion out of the cell**.
  * **Free Ribosomes (in Cytoplasm):** Synthesize soluble proteins for **use within the cell**.

---

### 7. Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum (Rough ER)
* **Structure:** Interconnected system of folded membranous sacs directly continuous with and "hugging" the nuclear envelope. Studded with ribosomes on its outer surface.
* **Function:** Synthesizes and folds proteins; packages proteins into transport vesicles destined for the Golgi apparatus.

---

### 8. Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum (Smooth ER)
* **Structure:** Tubular membranous network attached to the Rough ER; **NO ribosomes**.
* **Function:** Synthesizes lipids (phospholipids and steroid hormones); metabolizes carbohydrates; detoxifies drugs, poisons, and alcohol (abundant in liver); stores calcium ions (^{2+}$ in muscle cells).

---

### 9. Golgi Apparatus (Golgi Body)
* **Structure:** Stacks of flattened membranous sacs (cisternae) that are not physically connected.
* **Function:** Receives transport vesicles containing proteins from the ER; modifies, sorts, tags, and packages proteins into vesicles for secretion or delivery to lysosomes.

---

### 10. Vacuoles (Animal vs. Plant)
* **Animal Cells:** Multiple small, temporary membrane-bound sacs used for storage of water, nutrients, and waste products.
* **Plant Cells:** A single, massive **Large Central Vacuole** filled with watery fluid. Maintains **turgor pressure** against the cell wall to maintain structural rigidity and upright plant shape.
* **Protists:** Specialized **contractile vacuoles** that actively pump out excess water using ATP.

---

### 11. Vesicles
* **Structure:** Small, spherical membrane-bound phospholipid sacs.
* **Function:** Transport and secretion; safely shuttle materials between endomembrane organelles and the plasma membrane.

---

### 12. Lysosomes (Animal Cells Only)
* **Structure:** Membrane-bound vesicles containing acidic hydrolytic enzymes ($	ext{pH} pprox 5.0$).
* **Function:** Intracellular digestion; breaks down engulfed food, invading bacteria/viruses, and worn-out cell parts (**autophagy**). Can trigger programmed cell death (**apoptosis**).

---

### 13. Mitochondria
* **Structure:** Double-membrane organelle (smooth outer membrane, extensively folded inner membrane forming **cristae**, inner fluid **matrix**). Contains its own circular DNA and 70S ribosomes. Found in **BOTH plant and animal cells**.
* **Function:** Site of cellular respiration; converts biochemical energy from glucose into usable $.

---

### 14. Chloroplasts (Plant Cells & Green Algae Only)
* **Structure:** Double-membrane organelle containing stacked thylakoid disks (**grana**) suspended in fluid **stroma**. Contains chlorophyll, its own circular DNA, and 70S ribosomes.
* **Function:** Converts solar energy into chemical energy (glucose) via photosynthesis.

---

### 15. Centrosome & Centrioles (Animal Cells Only)
* **Structure:** A pair of barrel-shaped **centrioles** arranged perpendicularly to each other (	ext{ centrioles} = 1	ext{ centrosome}$). Composed of microtubules arranged in a ring.
* **Function:** Organizes the mitotic spindle fibers to separate chromosomes during animal cell division; organizes cilia and flagella.

---

### 16. Cilia & Flagella
* **Cilia:** Short, numerous hair-like projections from the plasma membrane. Help sweep food, filter mucus/substances (e.g., human respiratory tract), and propel cells. (Animal and bacteria cells).
* **Flagella:** Long, whip-like tail appendage (1–3 per cell). Propels the cell through liquid (e.g., sperm cells, protists like Euglena).

---

### 17. Cell Wall
* **Structure:** Rigid outer layer surrounding the plasma membrane. Composed entirely of **carbohydrates**:
  * **Plants & Green Algae:** Cellulose
  * **Fungi:** Chitin
  * **Bacteria:** Peptidoglycan
* **Function:** Structural support, protection, and maintenance of cell shape. Prevents plant cells from bursting in hypotonic environments.
