## 🎯 Unit Success Criteria

By the end of the unit, students will be able to:
1. **Identify organelles** based on descriptions of functions or visual images of structures.
2. **Compare and contrast** prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells.
3. **Make a claim** about the effects of different stimuli on cell organelles, supported by evidence and reasoning.
4. **Create an analogy model** that illustrates how organelles work together as a system.
5. **Distinguish between passive and active transport** given models or properties.
6. **Plan and carry out an experiment** testing how osmotic solutions will affect cells differently.
7. **Identify and label** the components of the cell membrane.
8. **Provide a verbal or written explanation** of how the structure of the cell membrane’s components allow transport of different types of molecules.
9. **Provide a verbal or written explanation** of how different types of transport contribute to maintaining homeostasis with named examples.
