🔬 Concept Breakdown & Quick Review

1. Solutions (Solute vs. Solvent)

  • Solution: A uniform mixture consisting of solute dissolved in solvent (Solution = Solute + Solvent).
  • Solute: The substance being dissolved (e.g., salt, sugar).
  • Solvent: The substance doing the dissolving (e.g., water). Water is the universal solvent because of its polarity.

2. Passive Transport (High $\to$ Low Concentration, No Energy / No ATP)

  • Diffusion / Simple Diffusion: Direct movement of small, nonpolar molecules across the phospholipid bilayer from high to low concentration.
  • Facilitated Diffusion: Movement of larger or polar molecules (e.g., glucose) through specialized protein channels without requiring energy.
  • Osmosis: The diffusion of water molecules across a selectively permeable membrane from high water concentration (low solute) to low water concentration (high solute).

3. Tonicity & Osmotic Pressure ("Dilute the Solute!")

  • Hypotonic Solution:
  • Lower concentration of solute outside the cell than inside.
  • Water enters the cell.
  • Result: Cell swells (and may burst / lyse in animal cells).
  • Hypertonic Solution:
  • Higher concentration of solute outside the cell than inside (e.g., 50% salt solution vs 10% inside cell).
  • Water leaves / exits the cell.
  • Result: Cell shrivels / shrinks (plasmolysis in plant cells).
  • Isotonic Solution:
  • Equal solute concentration inside and outside the cell.
  • Water moves in and out at equal rates (Dynamic Equilibrium).
  • Result: Cell size stays the same.

4. Active Transport (Low $\to$ High Concentration, Requires Energy / ATP)

  • Active Transport: Moves substances against their concentration gradient (from low concentration to high concentration); requires energy (ATP).
  • Molecular Pumps: Use energy and membrane transport proteins to pump ions (e.g., $Na^+ / K^+$ pump) across the membrane against the gradient.
  • Bulk Transport (Vesicle Transport):
  • Endocytosis: Process of taking large particles, debris, or food into the cell via vesicles.
  • Exocytosis: Process where vesicles fuse with the cell membrane to force large materials or waste out of the cell.
