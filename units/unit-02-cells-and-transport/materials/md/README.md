## 🧭 What This Folder Covers

This repository folder contains the complete, authoritative curriculum, study guides, laboratory data, teacher presentations, and interactive testing suite for **Unit 2: Cells and Cell Transport**, organized into two core sub-units:

### 🚚 1. Unit 2 (Part 1): Cell Membrane & Transport (GSE Standard: SB1.d)
* **Membrane Architecture & Chemistry:** Fluid Mosaic Model, phospholipid bilayer (polar hydrophilic phosphate heads & nonpolar hydrophobic fatty acid tails), cholesterol as a temperature-dependent fluidity buffer, and membrane carbohydrates (glycoproteins and glycolipids for cell recognition).
* **Membrane Protein Jobs (TRACIE):** **T**ransport (channels & carrier pumps), **R**eceptors (signal transduction), **A**nchorage (cytoskeleton & ECM), **C**ell recognition (MHC & blood types), **I**ntercellular joinings (tight, gap, desmosomes), and **E**nzymatic activity.
* **Selective Permeability:** Rules governing lipid-soluble nonpolar molecules ($O_2, CO_2$), small polar molecules ($H_2O$ via aquaporins), large uncharged polar solutes (glucose via GLUT transporters), and impermeable charged ions ($Na^+, K^+, Ca^{2+}, Cl^-$).
* **Passive Transport (0 ATP • High $\to$ Low):** Simple diffusion, facilitated diffusion, and osmosis down concentration gradients toward dynamic equilibrium ($\text{Net Flux} = 0$).
* **Tonicity Dynamics & Cell Responses:** Solute vs. solvent rules (*"Water follows solute"*); behavior of animal cells (lysis in hypotonic, normal in isotonic, crenation in hypertonic) vs. plant cells (turgid in hypotonic, flaccid in isotonic, plasmolysis in hypertonic; plant cell walls prevent bursting).
* **Active Transport (Requires ATP • Low $\to$ High):** Molecular pumps ($3\text{Na}^+$ OUT, $2\text{K}^+$ IN, 1 ATP hydrolyzed), endocytosis (phagocytosis cellular eating, pinocytosis cellular drinking, receptor-mediated endocytosis), and exocytosis (vesicle fusion and secretion).
* **Transport Labs & Quantitative Analysis:** Dialysis tubing semipermeable pore exclusion (iodine in $\to$ starch purple/black; glucose out $\to$ Benedict's test), gummy bear osmosis, and $\% \Delta\text{Mass}$ formula calculations.
* **Current Assessment:** **Passive Transport (PT) & Active Transport (AT) Quiz** and 55-Question Master Exam.

### 🦠 2. Unit 2 (Part 2): Cell Theory, Structure, Organelles & Microscopy (GSE Standard: SB1.a)
* **Cell Theory & Historical Discovery:** 3 core tenets (all organisms made of cells, basic unit of life, pre-existing cells via division); historical milestones of Hooke, Leeuwenhoek, Schleiden, Schwann, and Virchow.
* **Universal Cellular Features:** 4 universal structures shared by ALL living cells on Earth: plasma membrane, cytoplasm/cytosol, genetic material (DNA), and ribosomes.
* **Prokaryotes vs. Eukaryotes:** Evolutionary differences, size ($0.1–5\,\mu m$ vs. $10–100\,\mu m$), absence vs. presence of membrane-bound nucleus, naked circular DNA plasmids vs. linear chromosomes with histones, 70S vs. 80S ribosomes, and binary fission vs. mitosis.
* **Plant Cells vs. Animal Cells:** Unique plant structures (cellulose cell wall, photosynthetic chloroplasts, massive large central vacuole, plasmodesmata) vs. animal structures (centrioles/centrosomes, abundant lysosomes, cilia/flagella).
* **Comprehensive 17-Organelle Inventory:** Structure and function of Nucleus, Nucleolus, Ribosomes (bound vs. free), Rough ER, Smooth ER (lipid synthesis & detoxification), Golgi Apparatus (*cis* receive $\to$ *trans* ship), Lysosomes (autophagy & apoptosis), Peroxisomes (catalase), Mitochondria (cellular respiration & Endosymbiotic Theory), Chloroplasts (photosynthesis), Vacuoles (contractile vs. central), and Cytoskeleton (microfilaments, microtubules, intermediate filaments).
* **Endomembrane Flowway:** Compartmentalized protein synthesis pathway: $\text{DNA} \to \text{mRNA} \to \text{Ribosome} \to \text{Rough ER} \to \text{Vesicle} \to \text{Golgi} \to \text{Secretory Vesicle} \to \text{Plasma Membrane (Exocytosis)}$.
* **Surface Area-to-Volume Ratio ($SA:V$):** Mathematical geometry of cubical cells ($6/s$), metabolic limits on cell size, and cellular adaptations (microvilli, cell division).
* **Microscopy Principles & Optics:** Anatomy of compound light microscope, total magnification formula ($\text{Ocular } 10\times \times \text{Objective}$), field of view dynamics, image inversion, and focusing safety rules (coarse adjustment knob *only* on $4\times$ scanning power).
* **Kickoff Activity:** **Organelle Scavenger Hunt** (presentation & student packet lab).

## 🎯 Success Criteria
By the end of the week, students will be able to:
1. Identify and label the components of the cell membrane.
2. Provide a verbal or written explanation of how the structure of the cell membrane’s components allows transport of different types of molecules.
3. Distinguish between passive and active transport given models or properties.
4. Compare and contrast prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells.
5. Compare and contrast plant and animal cells.
6. Identify organelles based on descriptions of functions or visual images of structures.
