## 🌿 Plant vs. Animal Cells Comparison

| Feature / Organelle | Animal Cell | Plant Cell | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Cell Wall** | ❌ No | ✅ **Yes** | Rigid cellulose structure providing shape and protection |
| **Chloroplasts** | ❌ No | ✅ **Yes** | Site of photosynthesis; converts sunlight into glucose |
| **Large Central Vacuole** | ❌ No | ✅ **Yes** | Massive storage for water/turgor pressure; animals have small vacuoles |
| **Centrioles / Centrosomes**| ✅ **Yes** | ❌ No | Assist in organizing spindle fibers during cell division |
| **Lysosomes** | ✅ **Yes** | ❌ No *(rare/atypical)* | Digest cellular waste and worn-out parts with enzymes |
| **Nucleus** | ✅ **Yes** | ✅ **Yes** | Stores genetic material (DNA) and controls cellular activities |
| **Mitochondria** | ✅ **Yes** | ✅ **Yes** | Cellular respiration; generates ATP energy |
| **Ribosomes** | ✅ **Yes** | ✅ **Yes** | Sites of protein synthesis |
| **Endoplasmic Reticulum** | ✅ **Yes** | ✅ **Yes** | Rough ER (proteins) & Smooth ER (lipids); transport network |
| **Golgi Apparatus** | ✅ **Yes** | ✅ **Yes** | Modifies, sorts, and packages molecules for distribution |
| **Cell Membrane** | ✅ **Yes** | ✅ **Yes** | Regulates entry and exit of molecules (homeostasis) |
| **Cytoplasm** | ✅ **Yes** | ✅ **Yes** | Jelly-like fluid holding organelles in place |
| **DNA** | ✅ **Yes** | ✅ **Yes** | Genetic instructions for development and functioning |

## 🔬 Prokaryotic vs. Eukaryotic Cells

- **Prokaryotes (e.g., Bacteria, Archaea):**
  - **NO nucleus** (DNA resides in an unbound nucleoid region).
  - No membrane-bound organelles.
  - Generally smaller and simpler.
  - Always single-celled.
- **Eukaryotes (e.g., Plants, Animals, Fungi, Protists):**
  - **HAVE a true nucleus** holding the DNA.
  - Have membrane-bound organelles (mitochondria, ER, Golgi, vacuoles, etc.).
  - Generally larger and more complex.
- **Shared by ALL Cells (Prokaryotic + Eukaryotic):**
  1. DNA / Genetic Material
  2. Cell Membrane (Plasma membrane)
  3. Cytoplasm / Cytosol
  4. Ribosomes (protein synthesis)
