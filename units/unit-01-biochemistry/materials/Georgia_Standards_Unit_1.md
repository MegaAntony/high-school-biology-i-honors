# Georgia Standards of Excellence (GSE) - Biology
## Unit 1: Biochemistry & Cell Chemistry

### **Course & Unit Context**
* **Course:** Biology I Honors
* **Unit:** Unit 1 – Biochemistry (Macromolecules, Enzymes, Chemical Reactions, Properties of Water)
* **Weekly Focus (S1W3):** 
  * Describe the characteristics of all four biomolecules.
  * Identify the four biomolecules based on their properties and structure.
  * Laboratory analysis of water properties and macromolecule testing.

---

### **Primary Standard**

#### **SB1**
> **"Obtain, evaluate, and communicate information to analyze the nature of the relationships between structures and functions in living cells."**

---

### **Specific Standard Elements**

#### **1. SB1.c — Macromolecules & Biochemical Processes (Core Focus)**
* **Standard Description:**
  > *Construct arguments supported by evidence to relate the structure of macromolecules (**carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids**) to their interactions and function in the biochemical processes of life.*
* **Curriculum Alignment:**
  * **Carbohydrates:** Monosaccharides, disaccharides, polysaccharides; structure and role as immediate energy sources and structural support.
  * **Lipids:** Fatty acids, glycerol, triglycerides, phospholipids; nonpolar nature, long-term energy storage, membrane structure.
  * **Proteins:** Amino acids, polypeptide chains, folding/conformation, structural and transport roles.
  * **Nucleic Acids:** Nucleotides (sugar, phosphate, nitrogenous base); storage and expression of genetic information (DNA/RNA).
  * **Enzymes:** Protein catalysts, activation energy reduction, enzyme-substrate specificity, active sites, and factors affecting denaturation/activity rate (temperature, pH).

---

#### **2. SB1.d — Homeostasis, Water Properties & Transport (Foundational Context)**
* **Standard Description:**
  > *Plan and carry out investigations to determine the role of cellular transport (e.g., active, passive, and osmosis) in maintaining homeostasis.*
* **Curriculum Alignment:**
  * **Properties of Water:** Hydrogen bonding, high polarity, universal solvent capability, cohesion, adhesion, high specific heat, and density changes in solid vs. liquid state.
  * **Biological Significance:** How aqueous solutions facilitate metabolic reactions and prepare systems for cellular transport and osmotic balance.
