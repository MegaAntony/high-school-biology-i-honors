Unit 1: Biochemistry & Biological Macromolecules

Biology I Honors • South Forsyth High School • Standard: SB1.c

🎯 Unit Overview

Unit 1 focuses on the molecular foundation of all biological systems:
* The 4 Macromolecules of Life: Carbohydrates, Lipids, Proteins, and Nucleic Acids (monomers, polymers, bonds, and biological functions).
* Enzyme Kinetics & Catalysis: Active sites, substrate specificity, activation energy reduction, and denaturation factors (pH, temperature).
* Chemical Indicators Lab: Iodine (starch), Benedict's (reducing sugars), Biuret (proteins), Sudan IV / Brown Paper (lipids).
