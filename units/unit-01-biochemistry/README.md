# Unit 1: Biochemistry & Biological Macromolecules
### Biology I Honors • South Forsyth High School • Standard: **SB1.c**

---

## 🎯 Unit Overview
Unit 1 focuses on the molecular foundation of all biological systems:
* **The 4 Macromolecules of Life:** Carbohydrates, Lipids, Proteins, and Nucleic Acids (monomers, polymers, bonds, and biological functions).
* **Enzyme Kinetics & Catalysis:** Active sites, substrate specificity, activation energy reduction, and denaturation factors (pH, temperature).
* **Chemical Indicators Lab:** Iodine (starch), Benedict's (reducing sugars), Biuret (proteins), Sudan IV / Brown Paper (lipids).

## 🧪 Macromolecule Reference Cheat Sheet

| Macromolecule | Monomer / Building Blocks | Polymer / Large Structure | Key Elements | Biological Functions | Chemical Indicator Test |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Carbohydrates** | Monosaccharides (e.g., Glucose) | Polysaccharides (Starch, Glycogen, Cellulose, Chitin) | $C, H, O$ ($1:2:1$) | Fast/short-term energy, plant wall structure | • **Benedict's:** Orange/Red (Heat)<br>• **Lugol's Iodine:** Blue-Black |
| **Lipids** | Glycerol + Fatty Acids | Triglycerides, Phospholipids, Steroids | $C, H, O$ (few $O$) | Long-term energy storage, cell membrane bilayer, insulation | • **Sudan IV:** Red ring / dye layer<br>• **Brown Paper:** Translucent |
| **Proteins** | Amino Acids (20 types, peptide bonds) | Polypeptides, Enzymes, Antibodies, Receptors | $C, H, O, N, S$ | Enzymatic catalysis, cellular structure, transport, signaling | • **Biuret:** Purple / Violet |
| **Nucleic Acids** | Nucleotides (Sugar, Phosphate, Base) | DNA (double helix) & RNA (single strand) | $C, H, O, N, P$ | Storage and expression of genetic information | • Gel Electrophoresis (staining) |
