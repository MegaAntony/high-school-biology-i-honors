## ⚡ Current Week In Detail: S1W5 (9/7 – 9/11)

### 🎯 Weekly Learning Targets (GSE Standards: SB1.a & SB1.d)
* Identify and label all components of the cell membrane.
* Explain how the phospholipid bilayer and proteins enable selective transport.
* Distinguish between passive transport (0 ATP, high $\to$ low) and active transport (ATP required, low $\to$ high).
* Predict osmotic behavior in hypotonic, isotonic, and hypertonic solutions.
* Compare and contrast prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, and plant vs. animal cells.
* Identify organelles based on functional descriptions and microscopic images.
