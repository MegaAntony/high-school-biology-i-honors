## ⚡ Current Week In Detail: S1W7 (9/21 – 9/25)

### 🎯 Weekly Learning Targets (GSE Standards: SB1.a & SB1.d)
* Identify and label all components of the cell (prokaryote, plant, animal).
* Identify organelles based on functional descriptions or visual microscopy images.
* Construct claims about the effects of stimuli/toxins on cell organelles, supported by evidence and reasoning (CER).
* Model organelle systems and explain consequences when organelles cease to collaborate.
* Detail the organelles of the endomembrane system and their relay in protein synthesis.
* Predict osmotic behaviors across hypotonic, isotonic, and hypertonic solutions.
