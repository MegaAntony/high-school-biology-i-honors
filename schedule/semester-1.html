⚡ Current Assessment Window: Unit 3 Cellular Energetics (Oct 28 – Nov 2)

👉 Curriculum Focus: Unit 3A Enzymes & ATP + Unit 3B Cellular Respiration + Unit 3C Photosynthesis (GSE SB1.e)**

🎯 Unit 3 Success Criteria (Directly Tested on Fri Oct 30 MCQ & Mon Nov 2 Short Answer)

  • 3A Enzymes & ATP: Explain how enzymes lower activation energy ($E_a$) without changing $\Delta G$; distinguish lock-and-key vs. induced fit; interpret temperature, pH (pepsin $\text{pH } 2$, salivary amylase $\text{pH } 7$, trypsin $\text{pH } 8$), and substrate saturation ($V_{\max}$) graphs; compare competitive vs. non-competitive (allosteric) inhibition; model the ATP-ADP hydrolysis/synthesis cycle.
  • 3B Cellular Respiration: Trace carbon flow ($\text{Glucose} \to 2\text{ Pyruvate} \to 2\text{ Acetyl-CoA} + 2\text{ CO}_2 \to 4\text{ CO}_2$), electron flow ($\text{Glucose} \to \text{NADH/FADH}_2 \to \text{ETC} \to \text{O}_2 \to \text{H}_2\text{O}$), and energy flow ($2\text{ ATP}$ Glycolysis + $2\text{ ATP}$ Krebs via substrate-level phosphorylation + $32\text{–}34\text{ ATP}$ ETC via oxidative phosphorylation = $36\text{–}38\text{ total ATP}$). Explain why fermentation occurs when $\text{O}_2$ is absent (regenerates $\text{NAD}^+$). Diagnose the 4 mitochondrial poisons (2-Deoxyglucose, Arsenic, Cyanide, Oligomycin).
  • 3C Photosynthesis: Trace light-dependent reactions in the thylakoid membrane (PSII water splitting $\to$ ETC 1 $\to$ ATP Synthase; PSI $\to$ ETC 2 $\to$ $\text{NADP}^+$ Reductase $\to$ NADPH) and the Calvin Cycle in the stroma (RuBisCO carbon fixation, reduction to G3P, RuBP regeneration). Predict effects of PSII, PSI, and Calvin Cycle inhibition (STEM Case p. 15) and interpret the 9-step Algae Bead bicarbonate indicator pH scale ($\text{pH } 7.6\text{ yellow} \leftrightarrow \text{pH } 8.4\text{ red} \leftrightarrow \text{pH } 9.2\text{ purple}$, Lab Packet p. 16–17).
